# First steps

## Sign in

Open the URL of your Sessage installation and sign in with your configured account. Depending on the organization, this can be a local account or an Active Directory/LDAP identity.

After signing in, the main navigation provides access to your lists, global task overview, important tasks, **My Day**, search, recycle bin, profile and account settings. Enterprise installations can also show dashboards.

## Create your first list

A list is the central workspace in Sessage. It contains tasks, columns, labels and sharing settings.

1. Select **New list** in the navigation.
2. Enter a descriptive name such as `Website relaunch`, `Support intake` or `Back office team`.
3. Confirm with **Create**.

Enterprise lists can additionally use custom fields, forms, automations and email intake.

## Create your first task

Open the list and create a task. Use a title that describes the expected outcome. Add a description, owner, due date, priority and labels where useful.

![Task details with description, assignment and activity](/images/community/aufgabendetails.png)

Comments, attachments, subtasks and activity remain attached to the task, giving the team one traceable work history.

## Choose the right view

- **List:** compact overview and quick prioritization.
- **Kanban:** work organized by status or process stage.
- **Table:** structured comparison of many fields.
- **Calendar:** tasks organized by dates.
- **Dashboard:** reporting and cross-list oversight in Enterprise.

![Kanban view in Sessage](/images/community/kanban.png)

All views use the same tasks. Switching views does not copy or move data.

## Invite the team

Open **Share** in a list, enter an email address and select the appropriate role. Share links are also available. Enterprise can additionally grant access to individual AD users and AD groups.
