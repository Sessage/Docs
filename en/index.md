## What is Sessage?

Sessage is a collaborative task and workflow platform with two server editions. **Community Edition** is the open-source foundation for everyday work. **Enterprise Edition** builds on the same foundation and adds licensed modules for organization-wide workflows and oversight.

Community is not a limited trial. It can be operated independently and includes lists, tasks, multiple views, collaboration, notifications, the mobile app and AD/LDAP sign-in. Enterprise adds custom fields, forms, portfolios, dashboards, email intake, automation and directory-based sharing.
