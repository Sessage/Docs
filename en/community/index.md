# Community Edition

Sessage Community is the complete open-source foundation for tasks and collaboration. It is licensed under EUPL-1.2 and can be operated independently.

## What Community provides

- lists with columns and labels;
- tasks with descriptions, subtasks, comments, attachments, dates and assignments;
- list, Kanban, table and calendar views;
- email invitations and share links with role-based access;
- notifications, reminders, import, export and recycle bin;
- local accounts and AD/LDAP sign-in;
- REST API and the shared Sessage mobile app;
- administration for users, settings and personal access tokens.

![List view in Sessage Community](/images/community/listenansicht.png)

## Typical uses

Community works well for team backlogs, personal and shared task planning, project lists, editorial plans and other workflows that do not require custom fields, automatic intake or cross-list dashboards. Custom fields are available with Enterprise Forms.

## Open-source operation

Your organization controls the infrastructure, database, data location, backups and update timing. The source can be inspected and adapted under the terms of EUPL-1.2.
